# Neri Cash

**Know where your cash is headed, and what to do about it today.**

Neri reads your books, your bank and the messages your customers send. Every morning it tells you what needs attention, by when, and why.

**Get started: [neri-cash.fly.dev](https://neri-cash.fly.dev)**
Create your account, add your bank balance and your books, and your first brief is ready.

## What Neri does for you

- **A morning brief.** One short read, or tap **Play brief** to hear it. It tells you where your cash is headed.
- **Three things to do today.** Each one shows its dollar effect, the date to act by, and a ready-to-send **Draft email**.
- **It spots promises in your messages.** When a customer writes "we'll pay in January" in an email or on WhatsApp, Neri notices. It shows you the exact words and asks before it changes anything.
- **Ask Neri in plain English, or by voice.** Try "Can I make payroll?" or "What if I spend $10K in early January?"
- **A 13-week view.** See your cash week by week, with your lowest point marked. Switch to **If customers pay a week late** to see the risk.

## Get started in four steps

### 1. Sign up

Tap **Create account**. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters. You do not need to verify your email. Then type your business name and tap **Continue**.

Come back any time with **Sign in**.

### 2. Pick the kind of business you run

On the setup screen, choose the one that fits best:

- **Trading and import**
- **Restaurant and retail**
- **Services**
- **Something else**

Neri then suggests the tools owners like you use. You can also set the least cash you want to keep in the bank (**Keep at least**). Neri warns you before you go under it.

### 3. Add your bank balance

Neri needs today's real balance to start your forecast. When it asks, type the balance your bank shows today and tap **Save bank balance**. A negative balance is fine. Neri never guesses this number.

### 4. Add your books and messages

On the setup screen, tick the tools you use, or tap **Skip for now**. Ticking a tool tells Neri what you use; you add the data itself on **Sources**.

Then open **Sources**. Every tool works with a file today. Tap **Upload a file** and choose one of these:

- A spreadsheet export: CSV or Excel (`.csv`, `.xlsx`, `.xls`)
- A PDF, such as an invoice or a bank statement
- A saved email (`.eml`) or a text file (`.txt`), such as an exported WhatsApp chat

Some tools, such as Zoho Books and Outlook, can link straight to Neri once they are set up for your business. Each tool on **Sources** shows a label. **Connected** means it is working. **Connect** means you can link it now. **Coming next** and **Request** mean it is not ready yet, so upload a file instead.

Anything that could change your cash waits in **Review** for your OK. Until your data is in, Neri tells you what is missing instead of making numbers up.

## Your daily routine (5 minutes)

1. **Read the brief** on **Home**. Or tap **Play brief** and listen.
2. **Do the three things.** Tap **Draft email**, check it, and send it from your own email. Tick the circle when it is done.
3. **Check Review.** Apply or dismiss each message Neri found.
4. **Ask Neri.** Type or tap the mic. Ask a question, or park a thought like "new oven, about $9,000 in March". Neri shows what it does to your cash. It joins your forecast only when you tap **Add to forecast**.

## What Neri needs from you

| What | Where to get it | Add it from |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices and bills | Export from your accounting tool as CSV or Excel | **Sources**, **Upload a file** |
| Bank statement | Download a CSV, Excel or PDF file from your bank's website | **Sources**, **Bank statement** |
| Daily sales | Export from your till or POS | **Sources**, **Sales export** |
| Customer emails | Save the email as a file (`.eml`) | **Sources**, **Upload a file** |
| WhatsApp chats | In WhatsApp, open the chat and choose **Export chat** | **Sources**, **Upload a file** |
| Shipment dates | Export from your shipping company | **Sources**, **Shipment tracker export** |

## Safe by design

- **Neri only reads.** It never sends a message, pays a bill or moves money. It has no way to.
- **You approve every change.** Messages and uploads wait in **Review** until you tap **Apply change**.
- **You send every email.** Neri writes the draft. You send it from your own email.
- **Your data stays your business's.** Only people in your business can see your numbers.

## Questions owners ask

**Do I need my accountant?**
Neri does not replace your accountant. It helps you see the next 13 weeks and decide what to do today. Your accountant still looks after your books and taxes.

**What if a number looks wrong?**
Tap it. Every number links to the invoice, bank line or message it came from. If the source is wrong, fix it in your books and upload a fresh export.

**Does it work for a restaurant?**
Yes. Choose **Restaurant and retail** at setup. Neri uses your daily sales and your regular costs, such as wages and rent.

**Can Neri pay a bill?**
No. It never pays, sends or moves money. It tells you what to do, and you do it.

**What if Neri misreads a message?**
Neri tells you how sure it is of each reading. Nothing changes until you tap **Apply change**, and you can tap **Dismiss** instead.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. It works in your phone's browser. Tap **Drive mode** to listen hands-free.

**What does it cost?**
Neri Cash is a hackathon project, and it is free to try. There is no pricing yet.
