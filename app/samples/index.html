Sample files

No files handy? These are fictional (Cascade Trade Co.). First name your business "Cascade Trade Co." at sign-up and type a bank balance of 25,773.00 on Home. Then upload the files in your Neri account with Upload a file on Sources, in this order. After step 1, open Review and confirm what Neri read.

  1. 1-open-invoices.csv and 1-open-bills.csv: open invoices and bills from your accounting tool.
  2. 2-invoice-INV-1187.pdf: the same invoice INV-1187 as a PDF; Neri counts it once.
  3. 3-whatsapp-evergreen-chat.txt: a customer WhatsApp chat saying they will pay late.
